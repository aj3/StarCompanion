# Code of conduct

StarCompanion welcomes respectful, constructive participation.

- Discuss ideas and people with patience and professionalism.
- Give actionable feedback and assume good intent while evaluating evidence.
- Do not harass, threaten, discriminate, publish private information, or submit
  game data, credentials, personal logs, or exploit details publicly.
- Respect project safety controls, licenses, trademarks, and community rules.

Maintainers may edit, hide, reject, or remove contributions and participation
that violate these expectations. Serious or security-sensitive concerns should
use the private process in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Other conduct concerns may
be reported to the maintainer through the repository issue tracker without
including private information.
